HOW IT WORKS

A safety layer built for the underground

OREGUARD sits between the operator and the hazard — continuously measuring, computing, and intervening when conditions demand it.

01
Sensor Fusion

6 ultrasonic + LDR sensors stream data at 10 Hz to the ESP32 microcontroller. Forward, lateral, and rearward paths are all monitored simultaneously.

02
Visibility Analysis

An optical LDR pipeline quantifies ambient light attenuation to derive a real-time visibility percentage — the core input to the risk equation.

03
Dynamic Risk Engine

Risk is not a fixed threshold. The lower the visibility, the earlier the system escalates — warning the driver sooner and triggering adaptive speed limits.

04
Operator Response

SAFE corridors allow full speed. WARNING triggers audio and visual alerts. HIGH RISK cuts speed. EMERGENCY engages the automatic brake and halts the vehicle.
