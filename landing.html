ARCHITECTURE PIPELINE

A Safety Layer Built for the Underground

OREGUARD sits directly between the driver and the vehicle drivetrain — continuously sensing, evaluating, and intervening before human perception can react.

01
Multi-Sensor Fusion

6 ultrasonic sensors sweep 360° surroundings at 10Hz, while optical LDR sensors gauge ambient atmospheric clarity and MPU-6050 monitors haul road incline.

02
Optical Attenuation

Atmospheric particulate density from coal dust and mist is calibrated into a dynamic visibility coefficient, shifting safety thresholds earlier in poor conditions.

03
Dynamic Risk Recalibration

The edge risk engine dynamically calculates required stopping distance based on road grade, current vehicle velocity, and ambient visibility reduction.

04
Autonomous Intervention

Triggers multi-stage responses: Green safe path, Yellow speed advisory, Amber motor governor, and Red automatic emergency brake cutoff.

HARDWARE SPECIFICATIONS

Built on Industrial Edge IoT

Complete prototype hardware schematic designed for tough, dust-heavy underground mining environments.

ESP32 Microcontroller

Dual-core 240MHz MCU executing real-time FreeRTOS safety threads with sub-100ms sensor processing cycles.

LOCAL ZERO-LATENCY EDGE LOOP
HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Array

Multi-sensor proximity grid with running median filters to prevent echo noise and detect haul road hazards up to 4.0m.

DIGITAL 10Hz FILTERED SWEEP
LDR Optical Attenuation

Calibrated ambient light photocell analyzing light scattering from underground dust and blasting smoke.

ANALOG VOLTAGE DIVIDER PIPELINE
SSD1306 In-Cabin OLED HUD

0.96" 128x64 I2C driver cockpit micro-display rendering live visibility %, distance meters, and risk warnings to the operator.

IN-CABIN DRIVER TELEMETRY
MPU-6050 6-Axis IMU

Detects haul road incline, vehicle pitch, roll, and tilt angles to dynamically recalculate gravitational braking resistance.

ROAD GRADE COMPENSATION
Dual TT Motors & Auto-Brake

L298N motor driver with PWM speed regulation and instant emergency relay cutoff for automatic vehicle immobilization.
